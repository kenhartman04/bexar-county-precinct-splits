Bexar County Precinct Fragmentation

How precincts are split across congressional, state senate, state house, and SBOE district lines. Toggle district overlays in the layer control.

Methodology

Data Collection and Preprocessing:

  • Collecting data for precincts and district plans at various levels of government in Bexar County, Texas.
  • Reading shapefiles using geopandas and converting them to a common CRS (EPSG:2278).

Intersecting Precincts with District Plans:

  • Performing spatial intersections between precincts and district plans using geopandas' overlay function.
  • Calculating the area of intersection and fractional overlap for each intersection fragment.

Filtering Sliver Fragments:

  • Identifying and filtering out sliver fragments based on a specified threshold (sliver_fraction = 0.01).
  • Filtering out fragments where the fractional overlap is less than or equal to the threshold.

Counting Districts Per Precinct:

  • Counting the number of districts per precinct after filtering using pandas.

Building Comparison Frame:

  • Building a comparison frame to visually assess the impact of the sliver filter on district fragmentation across different levels of government.

Plotting Diagnostics:

  • Generating diagnostic plots to validate results and identify the optimal threshold for filtering sliver fragments.
  • Including a vertical red dashed line at the sliver_fraction threshold in the histogram.

Combining Results:

  • Combining all results from different levels of government into a single DataFrame for comprehensive analysis.
  • Saving output GeoDataFrames in GeoJSON format.
